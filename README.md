### Hi, I'm Ole 👋

I'm a 24-year-old computer science master's student at Leibniz University Hannover. Most of my courses right now are
machine learning in one form or another: deep learning, reinforcement learning, NLP, and lately language-model
agents. My favourite kind of project takes something from my own life and builds the ML version of it, which is why
there is a chess bot below that plays exactly like me, blunders included.

I grew up on Fehmarn, an island in the Baltic Sea, spent half a year in New Zealand at fifteen, and played drums in a
couple of island bands before I moved to Hannover for university.

**Off the keyboard**, you'll find me at the bouldering gym, on the bike, in the pool or out running, since I'm into
endurance sports and the occasional race. I took up chess during the pandemic and never quite stopped: I play a lot
of blitz, not particularly well (or you can [play my clone](https://ole-rgb.github.io/olebot-site/) and see for
yourself). At home I look after too many plants, play League of Legends, and own a Zuriga espresso machine that I
still can't get good latte art out of. Working on it.
